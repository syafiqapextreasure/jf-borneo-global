## AEO and GEO implementation

Publish important answers as crawlable HTML, not text inside artwork. Answer who the company is, what services it offers, where the two offices are, and how to discuss a requirement. Use descriptive headings, semantic sections, real image alt text, internal links, individual page metadata, canonical URLs, sitemap.xml, robots.txt and accurate Organization/LocalBusiness/Service structured data that mirrors visible content. Leave business hours and ratings out unless verified.

Useful visible FAQ answers:

- **What services does JF Borneo Global provide?** Manpower supply for offshore and onshore oil and gas operations, plus construction workforce enquiries.
- **Where are your offices?** Masai, Johor and Bintulu, Sarawak. Display the supplied full addresses and contact links.
- **What should I include in a manpower enquiry?** Project location, scope, roles and number of people, expected duration and preferred start date. Confirm availability and specific requirements directly with the team.
- **How do I contact the team?** WhatsApp or call 017-910 5885, or email jfborneoglobal@gmail.com.

Google states that its ordinary SEO practices remain applicable to AI Overviews and AI Mode, without special AI schema or extra machine-readable files. Search inclusion is not guaranteed. [Google Search Central: AI features](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features)

Do not promise FAQ rich results: Google's current updates say the FAQ rich-result documentation was removed in June 2026 because that feature is no longer displayed. FAQs can still help people understand the service. [Google Search documentation updates](https://developers.google.com/search/updates)

After deployment, use the actual domain for canonical URLs and sitemap, verify Search Console and keep business/contact profiles consistent. A language switch should link only to genuinely translated content, not an empty duplicate page.
